## Qualification conversation

1. What customer, contract or internal event triggered this review? What is the real deadline?
2. What specific evidence or work has been requested? Can we inspect a permitted/redacted example?
3. Who decides to buy, who can change DNS/application settings, and who reviews the result?
4. How is the work handled today, using which tools or advisers, and how many staff hours does it take?
5. Which requested items are external technical evidence versus internal policies, organizational practices, legal assessment or a formal audit?
6. What would count as a useful result in 30 days, including a scoped result with no actionable findings?
7. Would you pay for the agreed pilot now? If not, what is missing or what alternative would you use?
8. What would make you continue paying after the first period?

Record actual answers and disqualifications. Do not label verbal interest as revenue. If a critical need is outside Cyphward's scope, say so and determine whether an appropriately qualified partner can help.
